What is Typeshala?

Typeshala is the most popular Nepali typing tutor software, widely used in schools and computer institutes across Nepal. It builds muscle memory for the traditional Devanagari keyboard layout step by step — starting from individual vowels (स्वर वर्ण), moving through consonants (व्यञ्जन) and barakhari, and finishing with full paragraphs. The software tracks your speed (WPM) and accuracy after every lesson, and its built-in typing games keep practice fun for students of all ages.

Practice Tips सुझाव

  • Home keys first: rest your fingers on a s d f and j k l ; — return there after every reach.
  • Don't look down. Watch the glowing key on the on-screen keyboard instead of your hands.
  • Accuracy before speed. Keep errors under 5% — Net WPM climbs automatically once accuracy is solid.
  • 15 minutes daily beats 2 hours once a week. Your Best WPM is saved for every lesson.
